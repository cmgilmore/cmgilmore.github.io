Schedule a Free Consultation Learn More SCROLL ￼ Ed.D., LPCC, NCC About Me Before coming to the therapy space, I spent nearly two decades in organizational leadership, coaching and developing individuals and teams in a variety of settings. It gave me firsthand insight into burnout, achievement pressure, identity struggles, and the quiet way high-functioning people can sometimes appear successful on the outside while feeling anxious, exhausted, or emotionally stuck on the inside. Watching that gap between outward success and inward exhaustion up close, again and again (including, if I’m honest, in the mirror some mornings), is what eventually pulled me into counseling. My work today draws on trauma-informed and attachment-based approaches, including EMDR, to help people understand the why behind their patterns and restore a felt sense of regulation and calm in their nervous system. Not just insight, but an actual shift in how safe and settled life feels day to day. I bring the same direct, grounded presence that shaped my years in leadership into the therapy room, minus the mandatory team-building exercises. My clients are often navigating identity, belonging, relationships, and self-acceptance, and what I hear most is that they want a space where they don’t have to perform, where they can drop the “supposed to” and figure out what’s actually true for them. If we can laugh together along the way, even better. Growth doesn’t have to be humorless. Licensed Professional Counselor Candidate (LPCC), Colorado National Certified Counselor (NCC) EMDR-trained clinician ￼ How I Work Therapy with me is a genuine two-person project. You’re the expert on your life, I help guide the process, and together we figure out what’s actually going to help. Here’s some of what I draw on, depending on what you're working through: We’ll move at whatever pace actually works for you. Some sessions are quiet and reflective. Some get into the body. And some, without either of us really planning it, turn into you laughing at yourself for the first time in a while. Those ones tend to sneak up on you. Wherever we land, the goal is the same: not just understanding the patterns that keep showing up in your life, but actually feeling different because of them. EMDR An evidence-based approach that helps process and heal distressing experiences, reducing the emotional charge connected to difficult memories. TRAUMA-INFORMED CARE Understanding how past experiences shape present patterns, with deep respect for how the nervous system learned to survive. SOMATIC TECHNIQUES Bringing awareness to the body's signals and sensations as a pathway to processing and healing stored stress and trauma. MINDFULNESS-BASED APPROACHES Building present-moment awareness to create space between experience and reaction, and to reconnect with what matters. Frequently Asked Questions What can I expect from our first session? + Our first session is a chance to get to know each other. We'll talk about what brought you to therapy, what you're hoping to work on, and I'll answer any questions you have about my approach. There's no pressure. You set the pace. How long does therapy typically last? + That depends on what you're working toward. Some people find meaningful change in 8 to 12 sessions; others benefit from longer-term work. We'll check in regularly and adjust based on how things are going. Do you accept insurance, and what does a session cost? + I am a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. I can provide a superbill if you'd like to seek reimbursement from your insurance provider. Sessions are $150 for 50 minutes, and a limited number of sliding-scale spots are available. If Medicaid coverage is something you need, reach out and we can talk through your options together. Do you offer telehealth? + Yes, I offer telehealth sessions for clients in Colorado. All you need is a private space and a reliable internet connection. Do you work with couples? + Yes, I work with couples as well as individuals. Couples work follows the same collaborative approach: we'll talk through what's bringing you in, and I'll tailor the pace and structure of sessions to what's most useful for your relationship. What if therapy doesn't feel like the right fit? + It's important to work with someone you feel comfortable with. If after a few sessions it doesn't feel like the right match, I'll support you in finding a therapist who is. Your care comes first. ￼ Clinical Supervision I believe good therapists never stop learning.
