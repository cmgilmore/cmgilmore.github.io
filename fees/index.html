Why private pay?

I’m a private-pay practice and don’t bill insurance directly. For many professionals, that’s a feature: nothing is billed to your employer-sponsored plan, insurance doesn’t decide how many sessions you get, and a diagnosis is only shared with your insurer if you choose to submit for reimbursement.

Out-of-network reimbursement

If your plan has out-of-network mental health benefits, I can give you a superbill (a detailed receipt) to submit for possible partial reimbursement. Before starting, it helps to ask your insurer:

  • Do I have out-of-network benefits for outpatient mental health?
  • What is my out-of-network deductible, and how much of it have I met?
  • What percentage is reimbursed after the deductible?
  • Are services from a Licensed Professional Counselor Candidate under supervision covered?

HSA and FSA

Psychotherapy is often an eligible HSA or FSA expense. Check with your plan administrator to confirm.

Medicaid

If you need Medicaid coverage, reach out and we can talk through options together.

Good Faith Estimate

Under the No Surprises Act, if you aren’t using insurance you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of the expected cost of care. You can ask me for one at any time.

Cancellations

I’ll review my cancellation policy with you during intake.
